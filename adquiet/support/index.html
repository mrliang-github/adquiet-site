AdQuiet Support

Practical help for YouTube desktop watch pages in Google Chrome.

Start here: after installing or updating AdQuiet, reload the current YouTube watch page so the page can use the latest extension version.

Quick troubleshooting

01

Check protection

Open the AdQuiet popup and confirm that protection is enabled. If you just enabled it, reload the watch page.

02

Check the page

Confirm that you are using a standard desktop YouTube watch page. Shorts, Music, mobile apps, TV apps, and embedded players are not currently supported.

03

Try a clean reload

Reload the current YouTube watch page. If YouTube replaced the player during navigation, opening the video in a new tab can also help isolate the issue.

04

Prepare feedback

Note your Chrome version, what you clicked, and whether the issue affected an ad or normal content. The popup can copy fixed privacy-safe diagnostics.

Send feedback

Contact the developer on X. Please do not include passwords, account identifiers, private URLs, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Privacy

Read the English Privacy Policy or 中文隐私权政策. AdQuiet does not automatically send extension diagnostics with an external feedback link.

中文说明

AdQuiet 目前适用于 Chrome 中标准的 YouTube 桌面端观看页面。安装、更新或重新启用扩展后，请先刷新当前页面。仍有问题可通过上面的 X 链接反馈，但请勿发送密码、账户标识、私密网址、付款资料或其他敏感信息。

YouTube changes may temporarily affect AdQuiet. The extension reduces supported interruptions but does not promise permanent or 100% ad blocking.
