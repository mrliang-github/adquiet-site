英语练习

约老用户做产品访谈 · Asking a Long-Time User What Breaks Her Day

练习在「约老用户做产品访谈」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：A video call you booked with one of your users. It is evening on her side, and the call is only twenty minutes long — she has a client project due ton

目标：Interview a friendly user for twenty minutes, and turn polite answers like fine into one specific problem you can build for.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. pick your brain

    向某人请教、借用对方的经验（口语，语气客气）。

    You have shipped six courses already. Can I pick your brain about pricing for fifteen minutes next week?

  2. the last time

    上一次、最近一次（真实发生过的那一次）。

    The last time we talked, you said the export was slow. Is that still happening?

  3. run into

    偶然碰见；也指遇到麻烦、问题（不是计划中的）。

    Every new teammate runs into the same setup screen, so we recorded a short video for it.

  4. step by step

    一步一步地、按顺序地（讲或做）。

    Send the handover as a checklist, step by step, so the client can follow it without calling us.

  5. if you had to pick one

    如果只能选一个的话（虚拟语气，用来逼出优先级）。

    We can only redo one scene this week. If you had to pick one, which shot would help the video most?

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. You

    Thanks for making time, Dana. I know it is late on your side. I have wanted to pick your brain for a while. I am not selling anything today — I just want to hear how the tool fits into your work.

    谢谢你抽时间，Dana。我知道你那边已经很晚了。我早就想请你聊聊了。今天不是来推销的——我只想听听这个工具是怎么嵌进你的工作里的。

  2. Dana

    Happy to help. I use it every day, so I have a lot to say.

    很乐意。我每天都在用，所以有不少话想说。

  3. You

    That is why I asked you and not a new user. So, first question: which part of your day does the tool touch the most?

    这就是我找你、而不是找一个新用户的原因。第一个问题：一天里，这个工具最常出现在哪个环节？

  4. Dana

    The export, probably. Everything we make ends up in a client file, so I live in that screen.

    大概是导出。我们做的东西最后都要进客户文件，所以我天天待在那个界面。

  5. You

    Good. Can you tell me about the last time the export gave you trouble? Take me back to that day.

    好。能说说最近一次导出出问题是什么时候吗？带我回到那一天。

  6. Dana

    That was Thursday. I had four files from a client, and two of them came out the wrong size. I had to redo them one by one.

    那是周四。客户给了四个文件，其中两个导出来尺寸不对。我只能一个一个重做。

  7. You

    That is the part I wanted to hear. When you run into that, how often is it — once a month, or most weeks?

    这正是我想听的部分。你碰到这个情况的时候，多久一次——一个月一次，还是大部分周都会？

  8. Dana

    Most weeks, I think. Most days it is not a big deal. But on a deadline day it really hurts.

    我想大部分周都有。平时算不上大事。但一到交稿那天就很痛。

  9. You

    So on a deadline day it hurts. Now tell me step by step what you do when it goes wrong. Start from the moment you see the wrong size.

    所以到了交稿那天就很痛。现在请你一步一步讲，出问题的时候你是怎么做的。从你看到尺寸不对那一刻开始。

  10. Dana

    First I check the original file. Then I open the export settings and try a different preset. If that does not work, I rebuild the file by hand. It takes about twenty minutes each time.

    我先检查原文件。然后打开导出设置，换一个预设试试。要是还不行，我就手动把文件重建一遍。每次大概二十分钟。

  11. You

    Twenty minutes, and you would rather rebuild it than wait for me. That is painful to hear, but it is useful. One last question: if you had to pick one thing I fix first, what would it be?

    二十分钟，而且你宁愿自己重建也不等我。听着挺难受，但很有用。最后问一个：如果我只能先修一个，你会选哪个？

  12. Dana

    The size thing. Everything else I can live with. Oh, and let me know when you fix it — I will test it for you.

    尺寸那个。其他我都能忍。对了，修好了告诉我——我帮你测。

    I wish I could, but two months buys one feature, not three. So, if you had to pick one, which of them would save you the most time every week? That one goes into the next build, and I will bring you a rough version to test before it ships.
