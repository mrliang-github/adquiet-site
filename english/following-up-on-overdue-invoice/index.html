英语练习

催收逾期尾款 · Following Up on an Overdue Invoice

练习在合作方逾期未付款时坚定跟进，直陈事实并约定明确截止日期，同时维系长期合作关系。

场景：向海外合作方跟进拖欠六周的项目尾款

目标：催收逾期发票：直陈事实、锁定截止日，并保持未来续约关系。

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. past due

    已经过了该付款的日期，逾期的

    It is six weeks past due now.

  2. sort out

    把事情理清楚，弄出一个明确结果

    I just want to sort out one clear date.

  3. chase

    反复追问、追着催促

    I do not want to chase this every month.

  4. heads-up

    提前给人提个醒、打个招呼

    And just a heads-up — if it goes past that, I will have to pause.

  5. on my end

    在我这一方、我这边的责任范围

    Everything is ready on my end.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Nora

    Hi, good to see you. Sorry I am two minutes late — our team meeting ran long.

    嗨，见到你真好。抱歉我晚了两分钟——我们团队会议拖长了。

  2. You

    No problem. Thanks for making time today. I want to talk about the invoice for the June training. It is six weeks past due now.

    没关系。谢谢你今天抽时间。我想谈谈六月那场培训的发票。现在已经逾期六周了。

  3. Nora

    I know, and I am really sorry. Our finance person left in July, and things got messy after that.

    我知道，真的很抱歉。我们做财务的同事七月离职了，之后就乱了。

  4. You

    I appreciate that. I am not calling to blame anyone — I just want to sort out one clear date, so we are not doing this again next month.

    我明白。我打这个电话不是要怪谁——我只想理出一个明确的日期，免得下个月又来一次。

  5. Nora

    That is fair. I can send it to finance today. But honestly, they usually take about two weeks.

    这话说得对。我今天就能把它发给财务。但说实话，他们一般要两周左右。

  6. You

    Two weeks works. Let us say the tenth of October. And just a heads-up — if it goes past that, I will have to pause the next batch of videos for you.

    两周可以。那就定十月十日。另外先跟你打个招呼——如果过了那天，我就得暂停给你们做的那批视频。

  7. Nora

    Thanks for the heads-up. But pausing the videos? That sounds serious.

    谢谢你提前说。但暂停视频？听起来挺严肃的。

  8. You

    It is not a punishment. It is cash flow. If your payment is late, then I pay my editor and my server late too. And I do not want to chase this every month.

    这不是惩罚，是现金流。你们付款晚，我付剪辑师和服务器的钱也就晚。而且我不想每个月都来催这件事。

  9. Nora

    Okay. That is fair. And I want to be honest with you — the delay is not on my end. I have been chasing our finance team since July. Nobody approved it.

    好，这话说得对。我也想跟你说实话——拖的原因不在我这边。从七月起我一直在催我们财务。但没人批。

  10. You

    I believe you. I am not asking you to explain it again. I am asking for the tenth of October, and one line from finance if that date is not possible.

    我信你。我不是要你再解释一遍。我要的是十月十日；如果那个日期不行，让财务给我一句话说明。

  11. Nora

    The tenth is possible. I will walk it through myself. I am sorry it went past due at all.

    十月十日可以。我自己去走这个流程。很抱歉一开始就让它逾期了。

  12. You

    Thank you. A short note from you today would help a lot. Everything is ready on my end, so once finance moves, it is done. And I am glad we could sort this out.

    谢谢。你今天能给我写封短邮件说明一下就好。我这边全都准备好了，只要财务一动就结束了。也很高兴我们把这件事理清了。

MY TURN

轮到你说

  1. Nora 提出只能先付一半，剩下十一月再给且没有具体日期。请用英文回应：接受一半、对剩下部分要一个具体书面日期，并告知暂停视频的后果。

    查看参考表达

    Half now helps, and I will take it. But November is too open for me — can we put the fifth of November in writing today? And just a heads-up: if the rest is not in by then, I will hold the next batch of videos. I do not want to chase this again in December.

    先接受一半表达诚意，把开放月份锁定为具体日期，最后用 heads-up 和 chase 点明共同的边界。
