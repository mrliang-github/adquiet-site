英语练习

给海外剪辑师的改片反馈 · Giving Notes on the First Cut

练习在「给海外剪辑师的改片反馈」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：Thursday morning, at your desk. You have just watched the first cut of your launch video twice. Marco sent it last night with a link and one line: "ti

目标：Give an overseas video editor clear, specific notes on the first cut of your launch video — three changes, one night, and no room for a second round.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. heads-up

    提前打个招呼、事先提醒一句。

    Just a heads-up: I will be travelling next Tuesday, so anything you need approved should reach me on Monday.

  2. tighten up

    收紧、压缩，让节奏变紧。

    Can you tighten up the last slide? The same point shows up on it three times.

  3. swap out

    换掉、用新的替换旧的。

    We have to swap out the price in the ad — the discount ends on Sunday.

  4. hold off on

    先别做、往后放一放。

    Let's hold off on the paid ads until we see the numbers from the free test.

  5. on my end

    在我这边、从我这一侧看。

    The file opened fine on my end, so it may be a browser issue on yours.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Marco

    Hey, I just pushed the first cut. It runs about six minutes and twenty seconds. Send me everything you want changed at once — I would rather do one pass than five small ones.

    嗨，我刚把第一版传上去了。大概六分二十秒。你想改的一次全发我——我宁愿一次改完，也不想分五小次改。

  2. You

    Thanks, watching it now. Quick heads-up before the notes: the launch moved up to Friday, not next week. Is tonight still open for you?

    谢谢，我正在看。说意见之前先提醒你一句：发布时间提前到周五了，不是下周。你今晚还有档期吗？

  3. Marco

    Thanks for the heads-up. Tonight works — I have another job booked all day Friday, so tonight is my only window. Send me timecodes and I will send it back the same night.

    谢谢提醒。今晚可以——我周五整整一天都排了另一个活，所以今晚是我唯一的窗口。把时间码发我，我当晚就传回给你。

  4. You

    Good. Three things. First, can you tighten up the first forty seconds? It takes too long to get to the tool, and I think people will click away.

    好。三件事。第一，能把前四十秒收紧一点吗？进到工具画面的时间太长了，我觉得人会划走。

  5. Marco

    Sure. Do you want me to cut the intro, or just make it faster?

    行。你是要我把开头剪掉，还是只加快一点？

  6. You

    Faster. I still want the two lines about the problem — just tighten up the space between them.

    加快就好。那两句讲问题的我还是想要——只要把它们中间的空档收紧就行。

  7. Marco

    Got it. What is the second thing?

    明白了。第二件是什么？

  8. You

    The dashboard shots. We shipped a new version this morning, so those screens are already out of date. Can we swap out the four clips after the first demo? I will send you a fresh recording within the hour.

    仪表盘的镜头。我们今天早上发了新版本，所以那些界面已经过时了。能不能把第一段演示之后的四个片段换掉？我一小时内给你发一段新的录屏。

  9. Marco

    No problem. Anything else?

    没问题。还有别的吗？

  10. You

    Last one — let's hold off on the end animation. I am not sure the new logo is final yet, and I do not want you to do that part twice. The captions are already done on my end, so you can just drop them in.

    最后一件事——结尾动画先别做。我不确定新 logo 定下来了没有，不想让你那部分做两遍。字幕我这边已经做好了，你直接放进去就行。

  11. Marco

    That all makes sense. So: tighten up the intro, swap out the four clips, and hold off on the end. The audio sounds clean on my end, so I will leave it alone. I will send the new cut back tonight.

    这些都说得通。那就是：收紧开头、换掉那四个片段、结尾先不做。音频我这边听着是干净的，那我就不动它。今晚我把新版传回给你。

  12. You

    Perfect. I will send the new recording and the caption files within the hour. And if anything changes on my side, I will tell you before you start — I do not want you to do the same work twice.

    太好了。我一小时内把新录屏和字幕文件发你。如果我这边有任何变化，我会在你动手之前告诉你——我不想让你把同一个活做两遍。

MY TURN

轮到你说

  1. The new cut lands in your inbox in the morning, and it is good. Marco also cut a fifteen-second vertical version for Shorts, without being asked. He asks whether you want to keep it. The launch is still Friday. 对方说: "I also cut a fifteen-second vertical version for Shorts, in case you want it. Want me to keep it?" 你的任务: Answer him. Say whether to keep it, give him one specific change, and tell h

    查看参考表达

    Yes, let's keep it — and a quick heads-up: it would go out on Friday too, right after the long video. Two small things. Can you swap out the logo in the first second? That one is the old mark. And can you tighten up the first three seconds? Right now the hook lands at second five, and on Shorts people leave before that.

    先用 heads-up 把"周五也发"这件事单独讲出来，再用 swap out 和 tighten up 各给一条具体到"第几秒"的修改，最后附一句理由。三条意见全部落到可执行的动作上，对方不用再问一遍。
