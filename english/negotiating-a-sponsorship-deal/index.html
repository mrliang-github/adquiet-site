英语练习

与海外品牌谈视频赞助合作 · Negotiating a Sponsorship Deal

练习在「与海外品牌谈视频赞助合作」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：Tuesday morning, in your small home studio. Your camera is off, only the call is on. Your next AI tools video is planned for two weeks from now.

目标：In a first call with an overseas sponsor, agree on the money, the sixty-second mention and who keeps the final words — in English.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. a good fit

    合适、契合——两边合得来。

    Thanks for the intro — before we meet, can you tell me if this project is a good fit for my design skills?

  2. up front

    把话提前说明白、一开始就摆在桌面上。

    I'll be up front with you — I can finish the site in three weeks, not two.

  3. push back

    对某个要求提出异议、往回顶一下。

    The client wants it by Friday, but I'm going to push back on the deadline — two days is not enough for testing.

  4. in exchange for

    作为交换、以此换取。

    We can give you the tool for free in exchange for a written review on your blog.

  5. keep me posted

    有进展就告诉我一声。

    Keep me posted on when the new laptop arrives — I'll fly out as soon as it lands in the office.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Daniel

    Hi, good to finally talk to you. I've watched your channel for a few months. We just launched a new AI writing tool, and we would like to sponsor one video.

    嗨，终于跟你通上话了。你的频道我看了几个月。我们刚上线了一款新的 AI 写作工具，想赞助你一期视频。

  2. You

    Thanks, good to hear. Before I say yes, I want to make sure this is a good fit for my viewers. What do you have in mind?

    谢谢，很高兴听到这个。在我答应之前，我想先确认这件事对我的观众是否合适。你们具体是怎么想的？

  3. Daniel

    Of course. Two thousand US dollars. In exchange for that, we get a sixty-second part in the video, and our logo in the first minute.

    当然。两千美元。作为交换，我们在视频里拿到六十秒的段落，还有第一个一分钟里的 logo。

  4. You

    That part works for me. Let me be up front with you about one thing, though — my viewers come to me for my real opinion. I can't say a tool is great if I don't think it is.

    这部分我可以。但有件事我得跟你说清楚——观众来看我是为了听真话。如果我觉得一个工具不好，我不能说它好。

  5. Daniel

    I understand. That's why I picked your channel, not a big one. But our team would like to read your script before you publish it — just to check the facts.

    我理解。这也是我选你而不是大号的原因。不过我们团队想在你发布前看一下脚本——只是核对事实。

  6. You

    A fact check is fine. But I have to push back on one thing: if you can change my words, then it's not my video any more.

    核对事实没问题。但有一点我必须顶一下：如果你们能改我的措辞，那这就不是我的视频了。

  7. Daniel

    Fair enough. What do you suggest?

    有道理。你有什么建议？

  8. You

    Here's an idea. I send you the sixty-second part only. You check the facts and the numbers, and I keep the final words.

    我有个想法。我只把那六十秒的段落发给你。你们核对事实和数字，最终措辞由我来定。

  9. Daniel

    That works for us. One more thing — could you say our tool is the best one you have tried this year? That would really help.

    这个我们可以接受。还有一件事——你能不能说我们这款工具是你今年试过最好的？那对我们帮助很大。

  10. You

    I can't say that if I don't believe it. But I can tell my viewers what I really think after three weeks of using it — the good parts and the weak parts. That is more useful for you anyway.

    如果我自己不信，我就不能说。但我可以告诉观众我用了三周之后的真实感受——好用的地方和不够的地方。这样对你们其实更有用。

  11. Daniel

    You're right. Let's do it your way.

    你说得对。就按你的方式来。

  12. You

    Great. I'll send you a short plan this week — the date, the sixty-second part, and the payment terms. Keep me posted if anything changes on your side.

    好。这周我给你发一个简短的计划——发布日期、那六十秒的段落、还有付款条件。你那边有变动就跟我说一声。

You already told him the final words stay with you. 对方说: "Sorry, my manager wants to see the full video before you publish. It's just one email, right?" 你的任务: Reply to Daniel. Say your limit clearly, and offer one thing you can do so the

    查看参考表达

    Thanks, Daniel — I need to be up front: the final words have to stay with me, so I can't send the whole video for approval. But here's what I can do — you get the sixty-second part one week early, and your team can check every fact and every number before I publish. Does that work for you?

    先把不能退的那一条说清楚（up front），再用"我不能 X，但可以 Y"的句型给一个真的能落地的替代方案——对方要的是安全感和数据准确，不一定非要整片。用了本课两个表达。
