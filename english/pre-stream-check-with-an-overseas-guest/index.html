英语练习

连麦直播前的技术与流程核对 · Pre-stream Check with an Overseas Guest

练习在「连麦直播前的技术与流程核对」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：Fifty minutes before the livestream, in your small home studio. Only you and your guest are in the test call; the audience has not joined yet.

目标：In a short test call, confirm the time, the demo and the risks with your overseas guest — in English, before the stream goes live.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. double-check

    再确认一遍、复核一次。

    Before I send the invoice, let me double-check the billing address with you.

  2. heads-up

    提前提醒一声、事先打个招呼。

    Just a heads-up — the client moved the meeting to Monday, so we need the deck one day earlier.

  3. blur out

    把某处模糊掉、打码处理。

    I had to blur out my home address before posting the screenshot.

  4. run through

    把流程或材料从头到尾过一遍。

    Can we run through the agenda once before the client call?

  5. backup plan

    备选方案、兜底办法。

    The hotel Wi-Fi is slow, so my backup plan is to use my phone as a hotspot.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Alex

    Hey, can you hear me? My calendar says nine PM, but I want to double-check: is that nine PM your time or mine?

    嘿，能听到我吗？我日历上写的是晚上九点，但我想再确认一下：是北京时间九点还是我这边的九点？

  2. You

    Loud and clear. Let me double-check the time with you now — nine PM Beijing time, that is nine AM for you. Does that still work?

    很清楚。我现在跟你再对一下时间——北京时间晚上九点，也就是你这边的早上九点。这个时间还行吗？

  3. Alex

    Yes, nine AM works. Sorry, I always mix up the time difference. So, do you want me to share my screen for the demo?

    可以，早上九点没问题。抱歉，我老是搞混时差。那演示的时候要我共享屏幕吗？

  4. You

    Yes, please. Just a heads-up — your demo screen shows a test key at the top. Can you blur that out before we go live?

    要的，麻烦你了。提前提醒一下——你演示画面顶部有个测试的 key。开播前你能把那个打码糊掉吗？

  5. Alex

    Oh, good catch. I'll blur it out. Should I hide the repo name too? The feature is not public yet.

    哦，多亏你提醒。我会糊掉的。仓库名也一起藏起来吗？这个功能还没公开。

  6. You

    Yes, please keep the repo name off screen too. Let's run through the order one more time: intro, your ten-minute demo, then Q and A. Is that still okay with you?

    对，仓库名也别出现在画面里。我们把顺序再过一遍：开场介绍、你十分钟的演示、然后问答。这样你这边没问题吧？

  7. Alex

    That works. One thing though — the questions might be in Chinese, and I only speak English.

    没问题。不过有件事——观众的提问可能是中文，而我只会说英语。

  8. You

    No problem, I'll translate the questions and read them out in English for you. Also, can you send me your slides before eight thirty my time? I'd like to run through them once before we start.

    没关系，我会把问题翻译好，用英文念给你听。另外，你能在北京时间八点半之前把幻灯片发我吗？开播前我想先过一遍。

  9. Alex

    Sending them now. And what if my screen share fails? My Wi-Fi dropped twice last week.

    现在就发。那如果我共享屏幕失败了怎么办？我家的网络上周断过两次。

  10. You

    Good question. We'll have a backup plan: if your share drops, I'll put your slides on my screen and you just talk us through them.

    问得好。我们会有备选方案：如果你的共享断了，我就把你的幻灯片放到我的屏幕上，你直接口头带着大家过一遍。

  11. Alex

    Perfect, that takes the pressure off. See you at nine your time.

    太好了，这样我就没那么大压力了。北京时间九点见。

  12. You

    See you then. And if anything changes, just give me a heads-up — even five minutes before is fine.

    到时候见。如果有任何变动，提前跟我说一声就行——哪怕开播前五分钟说也没关系。

MY TURN

轮到你说

  1. It is four minutes before the stream. Alex messages you in the test chat: he now wants to demo a brand-new feature that is still private, and he asks you to stop the recording for that part. Your channel always keeps a replay for viewers. 对方说: "Can we show the new feature instead? It's way more fun. And please don't record this bit — I can't have it online." 你的任务: Reply to Alex. Give him a heads

    查看参考表达

    Hey Alex, quick heads-up — I can't turn off the recording, because my viewers expect the replay. But here's a backup plan: you talk us through the new feature without sharing your screen, and I'll blur out anything sensitive on my end. How does that sound?

    先用 heads-up 把"不能关录制"这个限制说清楚，再给 backup plan 让对方有台阶下——先回应限制，再给替代方案，比直接说 no 更容易被接受。用了本课两个表达。
