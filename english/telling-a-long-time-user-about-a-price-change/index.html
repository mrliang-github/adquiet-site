英语练习

向老用户说明涨价 · Telling a Long-Time User About a Price Change

练习在「向老用户说明涨价」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：A short video call on a weekday evening, your time. It is ten past nine. On your screen, next to the call window, an unsent email is open: the subject

目标：Tell a long-time customer about a price increase before the email goes out — say what changes, say what does not, and give her a clear date to act on.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. run through

    把一件事从头到尾过一遍、讲一遍。

    Before I sign the contract, can we run through the delivery dates one more time?

  2. bump up

    把价格、数量或等级往上调一点。

    We bump up the storage limit on the team plan next month, from fifty to two hundred gigabytes.

  3. grandfathered in

    老用户不受新规则影响，继续按老条件走。

    Everyone who joined before June is grandfathered in and keeps the free plan forever.

  4. specific to

    只针对某一类人或某一件事，不覆盖其他。

    This delay is specific to orders going to Canada — everything else ships on the normal schedule.

  5. keep me posted

    有进展就告诉我、别等到最后才说。

    Keep me posted on the shipment date — if it moves again, tell me the same day.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Priya

    Thanks for setting this up. I saw a note about pricing in the changelog last week, so I guessed this call was about that.

    谢谢你约这个电话。我上周在更新日志里看到一句关于定价的说明，所以我猜这通电话是说这个。

  2. You

    You guessed right. I wanted to run through it with you before the email goes out to everyone.

    你猜对了。我想在邮件发给所有人之前，先跟你把这件事过一遍。

  3. Priya

    Okay. So how much is it going up, and when?

    好。那涨多少，什么时候开始？

  4. You

    I wanted to tell you first, since you have been with us the longest: starting October first, we are going to bump up the monthly plan, from nineteen dollars to twenty-nine dollars. I know that is a big jump.

    我想最先告诉你，因为你用我们的时间最久：十月一日起，月付方案要往上调，从十九美元调到二十九美元。我知道这个跳得不少。

  5. Priya

    That is a big jump. Wait — does that mean my bill changes too? My team is five people now.

    确实跳得不少。等等——那我的账单也要变吗？我团队现在五个人。

  6. You

    No, and here is what does not change: you are grandfathered in. Your five seats stay at nineteen dollars, for as long as you keep the plan.

    不变。不变的是这些：你属于老用户保留旧价。你那五个席位一直是十九美元，只要你不取消这个方案。

  7. Priya

    Okay, that is a relief. But what about the two seats I wanted to add next month — for the new designer and the intern?

    好，那我放心了。但我下个月想加的那两个席位呢——给新来的设计师和那个实习生的？

  8. You

    That is the part I really wanted to run through with you. The new price is specific to new seats. So your five stay the same, and any seat you add after October first is twenty-nine dollars.

    这正是我最想跟你过一遍的部分。新价格只针对新增的席位。所以你原来那五个不变，十月一日之后加的任何席位都是二十九美元。

  9. Priya

    So if I add them this week, it is still nineteen? That changes what I do — I would rather not rush two hires just to save twenty dollars a month.

    那我这周加的话，还是十九吗？这会改变我的做法——我不想为了每月省二十美元就把两个招聘赶着做完。

  10. You

    Yes. Anything you add before October first keeps the old price. You do not have to decide today, but if you want to lock in the old price for those two seats, you have about two weeks.

    是的。十月一日之前加的任何席位都按旧价。你今天不用决定，但如果你想为那两个席位锁定旧价，大概还有两周时间。

  11. Priya

    Two weeks. That is tight, but at least it is clear. Can I ask why you are doing it now? The tool feels the same to me as it did in March.

    两周。是有点紧，但至少说得清楚。我能问一下为什么现在涨吗？对我来说，这个工具跟三月份没什么两样。

  12. You

    Fair question. The AI features cost me more every month now, and I cannot keep the same price and keep them running. I do not love the timing either, but I would rather say it to you straight than raise it quietly. I will send you the two dates in writing today. And keep me posted about the two new seats.

    问得合理。现在 AI 功能每个月的成本都在涨，我没法既保持原价又把这些功能继续跑下去。这个时间点我也不喜欢，但我宁愿当面跟你说明白，也不想悄悄涨价。我今天会把那两个日期写成文字发给你。那两个新席位的事，有进展告诉我。

MY TURN

轮到你说

  1. The call is almost over. Priya thanks you for telling her directly, then asks one more thing. Her business partner is opening a small studio of her own next month, and Priya can tell her to sign up this week. Same tool, same plan — just a different studio. 对方说: "One more thing — my partner is starting her own studio next month. If I tell her to sign up this week, can she get my old price too?" 你

    查看参考表达

    Thanks for asking — let me run through that part too. The old price is specific to your five seats, so a new studio would be on the new price. What I can do is send you a trial link today, so your partner can start now and lock in the old price for her first month.

    先承接对方的善意（她是你最早的付费用户，直接说"不行"太硬），再用 specific to 把范围划清——保的是"她那五个席位"，不是"她认识的人"。最后用 lock in 给一条她今天就能动的路。关键是别因为对方是老用户就把 to 越扩越大，那才是后面真正的麻烦。
