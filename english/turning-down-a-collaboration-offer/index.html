英语练习

婉拒海外合作邀请 · Turning Down a Collaboration Offer

练习在「婉拒海外合作邀请」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：A video call on a Saturday morning, your time. Dana booked thirty minutes. A slide with three bullet points is on the screen, and she is still on the

目标：Say no to a collaboration offer you actually like — clearly, kindly, and with one alternative left on the table.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. turn down

    谢绝、拒绝（一个提议、邀请或机会）。

    We turned down the first quote — it came in at almost double our budget.

  2. swamped

    忙得不可开交、被事情淹没。

    We are swamped with support tickets this week, so the blog post moves to next Monday.

  3. down the line

    以后、将来某个时候（不指明具体时间）。

    We are not hiring this quarter, but send me your portfolio — something may open up down the line.

  4. a good fit

    合适、匹配（人、事、机会之间的对得上）。

    This room is a good fit for a forty-person workshop, but not for a two-hundred-person meetup.

  5. up front

    坦白地、把话先说在前面。

    I want to be up front: the timeline you asked for is not possible at that price.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Dana

    Thanks for making time — I know it is late where you are. Half our team watches your channel, so I have been looking forward to this call.

    谢谢你抽时间——我知道你那边已经很晚了。我们团队一半的人都在看你的频道，所以我一直很期待这通电话。

  2. You

    That is kind of you to say. I read your email, but I wanted to hear the whole idea from you before I decide whether it is a good fit.

    你这么说太客气了。你的邮件我看了，但我想先听你完整讲一遍，再判断这件事合不合适。

  3. Dana

    So, here it is. We would love you to be our community ambassador for six months. One short post a week, one live session a month. In return, you get a free Pro license and your name on our site.

    那我来说。我们想请你做六个月的社区大使。每周一条短帖，每月一次直播。作为回报，你得到一个免费的 Pro 授权，名字也会挂在我们网站上。

  4. You

    Thanks for thinking of me — I mean that. But let me be up front: I am launching my own tool next month, and I am swamped until the end of November.

    谢谢你想到我——这是真心话。但我先直说：我下个月要发布自己的工具，一直忙到十一月底，事情多得做不完。

  5. Dana

    Oh. That is soon. Could you start after your launch? We are flexible about the start date.

    哦，那挺近的。你能在发布之后开始吗？开始时间我们是可以商量的。

  6. You

    It is not only the timing. Six months of weekly posts is a lot of extra work, and there is no budget on your side — so I have to turn this down. I do not want you to think I am saying no to you.

    不只是时间问题。连续六个月每周发帖是很大的额外工作量，而你们那边又没有预算——所以这件事我得拒了。我不希望你觉得我是在拒绝你这个人。

  7. Dana

    No, I get it. It is just... our budget this year is really small. That is why I hoped the license would be enough.

    不会，我明白。只是……我们今年的预算真的很小。所以我本来指望一个授权就够了。

  8. You

    I understand. So let me be up front about that part too: this is not a good fit for me right now, but it is not a no forever. Down the line, something smaller could work really well.

    我理解。那这部分我也直说：这件事现在对我来说不合适，但不是永远不行。以后有更小的合作方式，其实可能很合适。

  9. Dana

    Okay. So is there any version of it that works this year? Even a small one?

    好。那今年有没有哪种形式是可行的？哪怕小一点的？

  10. You

    Maybe one. I cannot do a weekly series, but I can do one paid workshop in January — two hours long, and you keep the recording. If that sounds useful, send me the details and I will look at it after the launch.

    也许有一个。每周连载我做不了，但一月份我可以做一场付费的工作坊——两个小时，录屏你们留着。如果这个有用，把细节发我，我发布之后再看。

  11. Dana

    That could work. I would have to check with my manager. So I should tell him you turned the big one down, and come back to you in December about the workshop?

    这个也许行。我得跟我的主管确认一下。所以我就跟他说那个大的你拒了，然后十二月份再来找你聊工作坊？

  12. You

    Yes, please. And Dana — if the workshop is too expensive this year, come back to me down the line. I am swamped now, but I am not going anywhere.

    对，麻烦你了。还有 Dana——如果今年工作坊对你们来说太贵，以后再回来找我。我现在是忙得脱不开身，但我不会消失。

MY TURN

轮到你说

  1. The call is almost over. Dana thanks you, then tries one more thing. Instead of the ambassador role, she asks for a single free post about their new feature — next week, which is your launch week. Same deal: no budget. 对方说: "Okay, forget the ambassador thing. But could you just do one free post about our new feature next week? It would only take you an hour." 你的任务: Answer her. Four things: thank

    查看参考表达

    I appreciate you asking, and let me be up front: this one is still a no. Next week is my launch week, so I am swamped, and one post means a full day of writing and recording for me. What I can do is put your new feature into my December video — or if you need it next week, I can send you our sponsor page and you can book a slot.

    先用 up front 把"还是不行"直接放出来，再用 swamped 给真实原因，最后用「I can't do X, but I can do Y」给两条可走的路（一个是未来，一个是花钱）。关键是不要因为对方说"只要一小时"就顺着答应——那是对方估的时间，不是你的。
