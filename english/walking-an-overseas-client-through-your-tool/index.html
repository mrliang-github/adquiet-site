英语练习

与海外客户做产品演示 · Walking an Overseas Client Through Your Tool

练习在「与海外客户做产品演示」场景下，用清晰、地道、专业的英语展开有效沟通。

场景：Wednesday afternoon, in your home studio. You are sharing your screen. The call is thirty minutes long, and twenty minutes are already gone.

目标：In a thirty-minute demo with an overseas client, walk her through what your tool does today, say clearly what it does not do yet, and still keep the deal moving — in English.

适合：独立开发者 / AI 内容创作者 · foundation · 12 分钟

KEY EXPRESSIONS

5 个重点表达

  1. walk you through

    带着你一步一步看一遍、过一遍。

    Let me walk you through this invoice in two minutes, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

  2. as of now

    就目前而言、截止到此刻。

    As of now, we only ship to three countries — I will tell you the day that changes.

  3. on the roadmap

    在开发计划里、被列进后续要做的清单。

    Bulk editing is on the roadmap for next quarter, but I will not sell it to you as if it is ready.

  4. narrow it down

    把范围收窄、把大问题缩成小问题。

    There are six things on your list — let me narrow it down: which two actually block you this week?

  5. double-check

    再核对一遍。

    Let me double-check the delivery address before we ship — it is cheaper than sending it twice.

DIALOGUE

完整对话

点击任一句开始点读。

  1. Maya

    Thanks for making time. We have used your free plan with the whole team for two weeks now, eight people. Before we talk about the team plan, can I show you how we work?

    谢谢你抽时间。我们整个团队用你的免费版已经两周了，八个人。在我们谈团队版之前，我先给你看看我们是怎么工作的，可以吗？

  2. You

    Please do. That helps me narrow it down — I want to see the two or three steps your team does most often, not the whole process.

    你说。这样我比较好把范围缩小——我想看你们团队最常做的那两三个步骤，不是整个流程。

  3. Maya

    Okay. First, we run about fifty images at a time for one client. Second, everything has to go into our Figma files. Third, each client should see only their own folder.

    好。第一，我们为一个客户一次要跑大约五十张图。第二，所有东西都必须进我们的 Figma 文件。第三，每个客户只能看到自己的那个文件夹。

  4. You

    Got it. Let me walk you through what the tool does today. Batch runs are in — I can show you fifty images in one go. Client folders are in too. Let me show you both.

    明白了。我带你过一遍这个工具现在能做什么。批量处理是有的——我可以一次跑五十张图给你看。客户文件夹也有。两个都给你看。

  5. Maya

    Good. Now the Figma part — that is the one our whole process depends on. Do you connect to Figma directly?

    好。那 Figma 这部分——我们整个流程就靠这个。你们能直接连到 Figma 吗？

  6. You

    As of now, no — and I would rather tell you that on this call than next month. The export is clean, but it is still a download and an upload.

    就目前而言，还不能——而且我宁愿在这通电话里就告诉你，而不是下个月。导出是干净的，但现在还是要下载一次、再上传一次。

  7. Maya

    That is a problem. If it stays manual, my team will not use it. That could be a deal-breaker for us this year.

    那就有问题。如果一直要手动，我团队就不会用。那对我们今年来说可能就是个过不去的坎。

  8. You

    I hear you. Can I narrow it down a little? Is the Figma link needed on day one? Or is it enough if the export is clean and your team can drag it in?

    我明白。我能不能把范围再收窄一点？Figma 直连是第一天就必须有吗？还是只要导出干净、你团队能直接拖进去就够？

  9. Maya

    Day one would be better. But if the export lands in the right format, we can live with it for a while.

    第一天就有当然更好。但如果导出格式对，我们暂时也能接受。

  10. You

    Then here is what I can do. For now, I will set the export to match your Figma setup, so it is one drag, not ten clicks. And I will put the Figma link on the roadmap — but I am not going to promise you a date today.

    那我能做的是这样。眼下我把导出调成匹配你们 Figma 的格式，那样就是拖一下，不是点十次。Figma 直连我会放进开发计划——但我今天不会给你一个日期。

  11. Maya

    That is fair. Let me double-check the export with my design lead tonight. I will come back to you tomorrow with a yes or a no.

    这样合理。我今晚跟我们设计负责人核对一下导出。明天给你一个明确的答复，行还是不行。

  12. You

    Perfect. I will send you a short note today, with the export settings and a screen recording of the batch run. If anything in your workflow changes, tell me — I would rather hear it early.

    好。我今天会给你发一封简短说明，包含导出设置和批量处理的录屏。你那边流程如果有变化，告诉我——我更愿意早点知道。
