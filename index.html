Chrome extension for YouTube desktop

Less interruption. More watching.

AdQuiet reduces video ad interruptions on YouTube desktop watch pages. It works locally in your browser and keeps its settings, aggregate counts, and limited diagnostics on your device.

Read privacy policy Get support

Local by design

Settings and aggregate statistics are stored locally through Chrome extension storage.

Focused access

AdQuiet operates only on YouTube desktop pages where its filtering features are needed.

No personal-data business

AdQuiet does not sell user data, build advertising profiles, or use your data for credit decisions.

AdQuiet is an independent extension and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
