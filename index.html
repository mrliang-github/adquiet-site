Focused by design

One extension. One clear job.

AdQuiet focuses on video ad interruptions inside YouTube desktop watch pages instead of requesting access to your entire browsing experience.

01

Pre-play filtering

Removes only known top-level ad fields from supported player responses and leaves normal playback data intact.

02

Quiet fallback

When an ad is clearly detected, AdQuiet tries the native skip action or quietly mutes, shields, and accelerates it.

03

Safe restoration

When normal content returns, your original muted state, volume, and playback speed are restored.
