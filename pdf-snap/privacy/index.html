PDF Snap Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Short version: PDF Snap does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. Imported documents and images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

1. Information PDF Snap handles

To provide document-processing features, PDF Snap handles the PDFs and images that you explicitly select. The app may store imported items, generated PDFs, operation history, temporary cache data, compression preferences, language preferences, and appearance settings locally on your device.

2. Photos, files, and email

  • Photos: PDF Snap uses Apple's system photo picker and receives only the photos you choose.
  • Files: PDF Snap uses Apple's system file picker and accesses only the files you choose and the export locations you select.
  • Email: When you choose to send feedback, PDF Snap may open the system mail interface. Content leaves the device only after you confirm that email.

PDF Snap does not request access to your location, contacts, or microphone.

3. On-device processing

PDF merging, compression, splitting, cropping, guide-line slicing, and image-to-PDF conversion are performed on your device. PDF Snap does not upload your source documents or generated documents to the developer's servers.

4. Network connections and third-party services

PDF Snap connects to Apple's public App Store lookup service only when you manually choose Check for Updates. Apple processes that request under its own privacy policy. PDF Snap does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. Sharing, tracking, and sale of data

The developer does not sell personal information, share it for advertising, perform cross-app tracking, or build advertising profiles. PDF Snap does not automatically send files, usage history, or diagnostics to the developer.

6. Retention and deletion

You can clear history and cache data in the app. Deleting PDF Snap removes data kept inside its app container. Files that you exported to Files or another destination remain there until you delete them separately.

7. Children's privacy

PDF Snap is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe a privacy issue involving a child has occurred, contact the developer using the address below.

8. Policy updates and contact

If these practices materially change, this page and its Last updated date will be revised. For privacy questions, email 228239753@qq.com or visit PDF Snap Support.

PDF Snap 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 31 日

简要说明：PDF Snap 不要求注册账号，不接入广告、第三方分析或用户追踪服务。您导入的文档和图片均在设备本地处理，不会上传到开发者服务器。

1. 应用处理的信息

为提供文档处理功能，PDF Snap 会处理您主动选择的 PDF 和图片，并可能在设备本地保存导入内容、生成的 PDF、处理历史、临时缓存、压缩偏好、语言和外观设置。

2. 照片、文件与邮件

  • 照片：应用使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，只接收您主动选择的照片。
  • 文件：应用使用 Apple 系统文件选择器，只访问您主动选择的文件和明确选择的导出位置。
  • 邮件：当您选择意见反馈时，应用可能打开系统邮件界面；只有在您确认发送后，内容才会离开设备。

应用不请求定位、通讯录或麦克风权限。

3. 本地处理

PDF 合并、压缩、分割、裁切、按引导线切片和图片转 PDF 均在设备本地完成。PDF Snap 不会把源文档或生成的文档上传到开发者服务器。

4. 网络连接与第三方服务

只有在您主动点击“检查更新”时，PDF Snap 才会访问 Apple 的公开 App Store 查询服务；该请求由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。应用未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。

5. 共享、追踪与数据出售

开发者不出售个人信息，不将信息用于广告分享或跨应用追踪，也不会建立广告画像。应用不会自动向开发者发送文件、使用历史或诊断信息。

6. 保留与删除

您可以在应用内清理历史和缓存。删除应用会清除其沙盒内的数据；已经导出到“文件”或其他位置的文件需要由您另行删除。

7. 儿童隐私

PDF Snap 不以收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息为目的。如您认为存在与儿童有关的隐私问题，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

8. 政策更新与联系

如信息处理方式发生重要变化，我们会更新本页面和最后更新日期。隐私问题请联系 228239753@qq.com，或访问 PDF Snap 支持页面。
