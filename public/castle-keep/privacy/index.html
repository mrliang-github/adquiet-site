Privacy Policy

Applies to: Castle Keep: Tower Defense TD (iOS) · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Short version: Castle Keep is an offline single-player iOS game. It does not require an account, does not collect or transmit personal data, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Castle Keep does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, account credentials, or contact lists;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or cross-app tracking data;
  • location data, browsing history, or behavioral analytics;
  • crash telemetry or third-party SDK diagnostics; or
  • payment or financial details.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Castle Keep stores your gameplay progress locally on your iPhone using standard iOS on-device storage (UserDefaults and local app storage). This includes unlocked campaign stages, wave records, earned medals, permanent Royal Tech upgrades, audio settings, and language preferences.

This game save data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to any developer server.

3. Optional Photo Library Permission (Add-Only)

Castle Keep lets you generate a 5:4 War Report card showing your battle results and share it via the standard iOS Share Sheet. Only if you explicitly choose to save that image to your Photos library, iOS will request add-only Photo Library permission (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription).

  • This permission is used solely to save the War Report image you requested.
  • Castle Keep never reads, scans, accesses, or uploads existing photos or videos from your library.
  • You can play the entire game without granting Photo Library access.

4. Third-Party Services and Network Use

All game logic and 3D assets are bundled inside the app so Castle Keep runs completely offline. The app contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or social tracking scripts.

If you manually choose to share a War Report card via the iOS Share Sheet or email support from your own device, that action is handled directly by iOS and the app or email provider you choose.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all game progress is stored solely on your device, your save data remains on your iPhone until you reset progress or uninstall Castle Keep. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data in accordance with iOS behavior.

6. Children's Privacy

Castle Keep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

7. Changes to This Policy

If Castle Keep's privacy practices change in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date before those changes take effect.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at mrliang8816@gmail.com or visit the Castle Keep Support page.
