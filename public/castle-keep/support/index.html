Frequently Asked Questions

01

Offline play & local saves

Castle Keep runs 100% offline with no sign-in required. Your campaign stages, medals, and Permanent Royal Tech upgrades are saved locally on your iPhone. Please note that uninstalling the app removes local save data.

02

Switching game language

Castle Keep starts in English by default. Tap the gear (Settings) icon in the top-right corner of the battlefield at any time to switch between English and Simplified Chinese.

03

Saving War Report cards

Tap "Share Best Record" to open the iOS Share Sheet and save or send your 5:4 War Report card. If saving to Photos was previously declined, enable "Add Photos Only" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Castle Keep.

04

Audio & performance

Game sound respects your iPhone's silent switch and allows background music to keep playing. If performance drops on a late Endless wave, toggle 1x speed or close background apps.

Report an Issue

Send an email to mrliang8816@gmail.com with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Castle Keep app version (for example, v1.0); and
  • the stage, wave number, and a brief description of what happened.
