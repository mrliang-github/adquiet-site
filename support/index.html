AdQuiet Support

AdQuiet is designed for YouTube desktop watch pages in Google Chrome.

Quick troubleshooting

  1. Open the AdQuiet extension popup and confirm that AdQuiet is enabled.
  2. Reload the current YouTube watch page after installing or updating the extension.
  3. Confirm that you are using a standard YouTube desktop watch page.
  4. If the issue continues, note the browser version and the action that caused the problem.

Send feedback

Contact the developer on X. Please do not include passwords, account identifiers, private URLs, or other sensitive information.
